title: What is Tool Ninja?
description: An introduction to Tool Ninja and its Premiere Pro and After Effects extensions.
---

Tool Ninja builds **focused Premiere Pro and After Effects extensions** that live right in your workflow — no round-trips, no bloat. Each tool does one job well and runs as a docked panel inside the app you already use.

## The tools

- **CutCap** — a caption editor for Premiere Pro. Import an SRT, VTT, or CSV, refine the cut and the wording word-by-word right in the panel, then push the finished captions back onto your timeline as a caption track or as Motion Graphics Template clips. [Read the CutCap docs](/toolninja-docs/cutcap/overview/).

More tools are on the way — their guides will appear in this documentation as they ship.

## What you get

- **One-time purchase, yours forever.** No subscription.
- **Works offline** after a one-time license activation.
- **Your files never leave your machine** — everything runs locally inside Premiere.
- **A UI that matches Premiere** — dark by default, with an optional light theme and a customizable accent color.

## Requirements

Tool Ninja extensions are **Windows-only** in their current versions and run inside **Adobe Premiere Pro 2023–2026** (CEP panels, v23.0+). Per-tool requirements are noted on each tool's overview page.
