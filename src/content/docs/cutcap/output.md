When your captions are ready, CutCap gets them back into your edit — to disk as an SRT, or straight onto the timeline.

## History

CutCap **auto-saves** your work continuously. The **History** panel reopens your recent edits, so you can close the panel mid-edit and pick up exactly where you left off.

On launch, CutCap can **reopen your previous session**, **start fresh**, or **ask** each time. Set this in **☰ → Settings → On launch**.

## Export SRT

**Export SRT** saves the edited file to disk — you choose where. Use this any time you want a plain SRT copy.

## Push to Premiere

**Push to Premiere** places the captions on a **new caption track** on your sequence, and files a **versioned SRT copy** — named after your sequence, e.g. `Sequence_v2.srt` — in a **CutCap** bin in the Project panel.

### Push scope

Choose to push:

- **Everything** — all captions in the file, or
- **In / Out range** — only the captions within the sequence's In/Out points.

### Where the file is saved

The SRT is saved to a **CutCap** folder beside your project file by default. You can set your own folder in **☰ → Settings → Caption save location**.
