## Does CutCap generate captions or transcribe audio?

No — CutCap **edits caption files you already have** (from Premiere's speech-to-text, a transcription service, or anywhere else that gives you an SRT). It makes fixing them fast.

## Does it work on Mac?

Not in v1 — **Windows only**. If that changes, it'll be announced on the [Tool Ninja site](https://toolninja.framer.website/) and here.

## Which Premiere versions are supported?

**Premiere Pro 2023 through 2026** (CEP panel, v23.0+). Tested on Premiere Pro 2026.

## Is it a subscription?

No. **One-time purchase, yours forever** (see the EULA for update terms).

## How many computers can I install it on?

**Up to 2 computers** that you personally use — one license per person. Replaced a machine and hit the limit? Email support and we'll reset it. See [Activating your license](/toolninja-docs/getting-started/licensing/).

## The installer says "self-signed" — is that safe?

Yes, that's **normal for independent Premiere extensions** — it means the package is signed by an independent developer rather than by Adobe. The included install guide walks you through it. See [Installing an extension](/toolninja-docs/getting-started/installation/).

## Something's not working — where do I get help?

Email [toolninjaa@gmail.com](mailto:toolninjaa@gmail.com) with your Premiere version and a screenshot. The download also includes a troubleshooting guide covering the common cases.
