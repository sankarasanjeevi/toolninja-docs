Version history for **CutCap**.

## 1.2.0

- **Push as MOGRTs** with a template gallery, folders, and auto-rendered previews.
- **VTT and CSV import**, plus whole-folder import.
- **Add and delete captions** anywhere in the list.
- **Cleanup bar** — remove gaps, line breaks, and punctuation across the whole file.
- **In / Out push scope** — push everything or only the sequence's In/Out range.
- Optional **light theme** and a new **brand accent color**.

## 1.1.1

- Fixed Settings, About, and History panels extending past the panel bottom at UI sizes above 100%.

## 1.1.0

- **License activation** — one license, up to 2 computers, works offline after activation.
- **UI-size control** that scales the whole panel.

## 1.0.0

- Initial release: SRT import, word-level editing, click-to-seek and Sync mode, Find & Replace, flags, undo/redo, auto-saved sessions, push to Premiere, and SRT export.
