Version history for **Subtext**.

:::note[Why the version restarts at 1.0.0]
Subtext was previously called **CutCap**. The version number restarts at 1.0.0 with the rename so
that everywhere Subtext is sold shares one numbering. The releases below the line are the same
product's history under its old name — CutCap 1.1.1 is the version this release replaces.
:::

## 1.0.0 — first release as Subtext

- **Renamed** — CutCap is now Subtext. Same tool, same developer. Your license key still works, and
  this version installs over CutCap keeping your saved sessions and settings. The panel appears as
  **Subtext** under Window → Extensions, and pushed SRTs are filed in a **Subtext** bin.
- **macOS support** — Subtext now runs on Mac as well as Windows.
- **VTT and CSV import**, plus whole-folder import. A CSV with frame timecodes asks for your frame
  rate once.
- **Add and delete captions** anywhere in the list.
- **Cleanup bar** — remove gaps, line breaks, and punctuation across the whole file, all undoable.
- **In / Out push scope** — push everything or only the sequence's In/Out range.
- Optional **light theme** and a new **brand accent color**, still fully customizable in Settings.
- Keyboard flow: Up/Down walk the captions with the playhead following, Enter edits, F flags.
- Find & Replace fixes: the arrow-nav match highlight is back, and the match count updates after
  an undo.

## Previously released as CutCap

### CutCap 1.1.1

- Fixed Settings, About, and History panels extending past the panel bottom at UI sizes above 100%.

### CutCap 1.1.0

- **License activation** — one license, up to 2 computers, works offline after activation.
- **UI-size control** that scales the whole panel.

### CutCap 1.0.0

- Initial release: SRT import, word-level editing, click-to-seek and Sync mode, Find & Replace,
  flags, undo/redo, auto-saved sessions, push to Premiere, and SRT export.
